## Recording Delivery Tips

1. **Pacing:** Maintain an even, calm pace. Do not rush. Let the screen action breathe during pauses.
2. **Audio Setup:** Speak roughly 6 inches away from your microphone at a 45-degree angle to prevent plosives ("p" and "b" popping).
3. **Breathing:** Natural pauses are marked with paragraph breaks. Take a quiet breath before each section.
4. **Energy Level:** Sound enthusiastic about the agricultural social impact without sounding like a television infomercial.
